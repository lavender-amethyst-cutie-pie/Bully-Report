# Project brief: Bully Report

## The competition

- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Middle school (grades 6–8)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: I can't miss the registration deadline and the project submission deadline.

## The problem

- People in Eastvale schools struggle with acting kind because there are multiple reports showing bullying or harassments from students.
- Impact area: Safety and kindness
- Why it matters (impact): Every school should have a website where they will report every bullying they expierienced or saw. Once they submit, that description will go to the admin or principle's desktop, so they can tell parents or legal guardians to deal with the issue.
- Evidence it’s real: One fact is that from recent posts in 2021-2022, 40% of 7th graders expierienced harassment and bullying, along with 9th and 11th graders. Link: https://nces.ed.gov/programs/coe/indicator/a10?tid=4
- What people told me: P1 said that the harassment towards them happened every single day. P2 said something similar but was faced with physical bullying. With these, I can think of ways to build my project and ask more people with the same problem. P1 said if they could stop saying responses with harassment of nationality or threats of religion, he would finally be able feel better. P2 said somwthing almost as the same but without physical attacks.

## My solution

- My AI’s job: My AI helps by determining whether the user's description is a real threat or a minor thing, which works better than telling the teacher of most threats because dealing it themselves doesn't work, and also, most students doesn't know what real threats of silly jokes are made for fun.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: There are websites with the same "stop bullying" program. One is the CNUSD Say Something Anonymous Reporting System. Another is the CNUSD Gaggle Safety Management. Both are websites that stops bullying, but mine will have a real AI that determines whether the comments are real serious or silly jokes.
- Safety plan: Risk: the AI gives the wrong answer. Plan: make it suggest telling the teacher to double check. Risk: user adds the bully's full name. Plan: tell the user only to put the bully's first name, and grade If they don't know, tell them to put the teacher's name.

## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)

1. The user can describe a situation involving bullying or harassment.
2. The user can be prompted to tell a teacher if needed.
3. The user can add the grade of the person involved.
4. The user can input the teacher's name if they don't know the bully's grade.
5. The user can input only the bully's first name and grade.
6. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.

## My AI’s instructions

- When it answers: The user types in the words or actions of the threat.
- Its job: To determine whether the user's input is a real threat or a joke.
- Who it talks to and how to sound: The AI is talking to the user by responding in word.
- Facts it must use: The AI must use real laws that connects to bullying.
- Rules it must follow: Make fun of the user or say anything negative, offending, or anything not related to the topic.
- How to answer: The answer can't be way too long. I don't want a lecture. Respond about 4 sentences. First let the AI respond asking questions about the threat. If the final answer is pasted, let the AI think and respond what to do.
